Go-to-Market Strategy

Internship

Rituals was already an established and iconic European bath and beauty brand, with a recognized identity and a large consumer base familiar with its products. Entering Southeast Asia meant protecting that identity while accounting for local regulations, product requirements, and a market that shops differently from Europe.

01
Built the go-to-market strategy Sized the market, mapped the competitive landscape, and defined the segmentation and channel strategy behind Rituals' Southeast Asia entry.
02
Mapped Southeast Asian consumer behavior Benchmarked how the region shops for beauty, online and in store, against Rituals' 1,000+ store European base.
03
Presented findings to the global strategy team Shaped the regional launch approach around local regulations, product certifications, labeling, and campaign messaging adapted within global brand guidelines.

Digital & E-Commerce

Marketing Executive

Beyond opening physical stores, we launched localized websites for Malaysia and Singapore and laid the foundation for an omnichannel path-to-purchase strategy across both markets.

01
Launched localized e-commerce sites Worked with engineering, e-commerce, and merchandising to ship sites for Malaysia and Singapore.

Approach

Two distinct audiences. Tailored messaging for brand-aware European travelers and new-to-Rituals shoppers.

Awareness and conversion. Targeted campaigns to build awareness with new customers and convert existing ones.

Online into in-store. Bridged digital and physical touchpoints so online discovery drove store visits and purchases.

Impact & Reflection

From 0 to 10+ stores across three countries

Today, Rituals has more than 10 store locations across Malaysia, Singapore, and Thailand — built on the go-to-market strategy and launch playbook developed during the initial expansion. This experience taught me what it means to own a product from zero: deeply understanding your market, aligning cross-functional stakeholders around a shared vision, and moving fluidly between research, strategy, and execution to deliver results under real constraints.
